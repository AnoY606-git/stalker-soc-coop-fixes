# Bar and Rostok: the Barkeep jobs, the hunter and Kruglov of the original SoC (phase 2)

Request (maintainer FOSTER): "the quests themselves must be remade to match the original SoC". Phase 1 (texts) is `patches\soc_task_texts`; its NOTES.md section "Phase 2" listed the differences of the Bar and Rostok. This patch does their structure. Dark Valley / X18 and the first half of the Barkeep dialog are `patches\soc_dv_x18_quests` (this patch extends that dialog, see Files).

## The original SoC (Steam SoC 1.0006: tasks_bar.xml, info_l07rostok.xml, dialogs_bar.xml, bar_dialogs.script, bar_rostok_tasks.script, xr_effects.script)
1. Barkeep (`bar_bar_barmen_start`), after the X18 documents: speech 1721 ... 17213 "Yeah." gives `bar_x16_documents_start` = task `bar_x16_documents` "Find the documents in X16 lab" (objectives: talk to the scientists, find the X16 documents `yan_x16_documents_gain`, bring them to the Barkeep).
2. The documents come from Ghost's body in the X16 lab. Barkeep 12 / 121 / 1211: "Yeah. Here." takes `lab_x16_documents`, gives `bar_x16_documents_done`. SoC pays NOTHING there (`bar_dialogs.give_x16_reward` exists but no phrase calls it).
3. Barkeep 13 / 131 / 1312: the Brain Scorcher job, gives `bar_deactivate_radar_start` = task `bar_radar_deactivate` (reach the antennae, find the bunker, switch it off). Only after step 2. The Military level is NOT part of the Barkeep's job (it is on the way to the Radar, its quests are optional).
4. Hunter (`bar_bar_hunter_start`, `_quest_start`, `_quest_finish`): task `bar_hunter_rifle` (find the family rifle, return it to the hunter at the Bar), the hunter pays 4000.
5. Kruglov (`bar_resque_information` "Meet Kruglov": get through to Kruglov, talk to professor Kruglov; `bar_resque_information_2` "Lead Kruglov to Yantar": lead him to the exit to Yantar, talk to Kruglov).
6. Duty / Freedom line: see the last section.

## How it works
`coop_soc_barros.script` (loaded by `coop_scn_soc_barros.script`, like every `coop_scn_*`): a server tick (1 s), the pattern of `coop_soc_dv.script`.
1. `progress()` derives flags from the world: any living player holds `lab_x16_documents` (or Ghost's documents were taken, `X16_GHOST_DOCS_TAKEN`, or the X16 psi installation is off) -> `YAN_X16_DOCUMENTS_GAIN`; a player holds the hunter's rifle after the quest was accepted -> `BAR_HUNTER_RIFLE_FOUND`; the X-10 switch is off -> `BAR_DEACTIVATE_RADAR_DONE`; reaching Yantar -> `BAR_KRUGLOV_FOLLOW_ACTOR`. Later flags set the earlier SoC ones.
2. Every task whose stage is open is given to EVERY living player once per level load (`GiveTaskToClient`; the stock `give_task` hands a task to the first player only). A stage closes with the same flag that completes its task in `tm_coop_l05_bar.ltx` / `tm_coop_l06_rostok.ltx`, so a finished task never returns.
Soft-lock rules: the Barkeep hand-ins check the talking player's inventory (X16 documents, the hunter's rifle), but open to the talker when the party took the item once and nobody carries it any more (a player died with it) or the X16 lab was finished another way; a dead Kruglov or hunter cannot block anything (every wait is a flag, Kruglov is invulnerable in the mod); every dialog action and its flag are in the same phrase.
