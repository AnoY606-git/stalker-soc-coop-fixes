# SoC main menu - notes

FOSTER asked whether the original Shadow of Chernobyl main menu could be ported. (`patches\soc_music` already restored the SoC menu music.)

## What the menu is (Shadow of COOP 8.x on xrMPE 0.9)
- Drawn by xrMPE script + xml, not by closed engine code for the look: `scripts\ui_main_menu.script` (the mod's copy only removes the "buy STALKER games" news block of the base xrMPE script) builds `configs\ui\ui_mm_main.xml`.
- The mod's xml (identical to xrMPE's except for the news block) shows a modern animated video background (`textures\ui\menu_background_*.ogm`, the 1080p Cordon night scene), the logo "Shadow of COOP / ARCANUM Add-on" (`ui_main_menu_logos.dds`), a title line and plain white 320x42 fade buttons.
- Pages (all kept): `menu_main` (Play = btn_net_game, Options, Credits, Exit), `menu_main_logout`, `menu_main_mm`, `menu_main_coop_mm` (the in-game ESC menu: Return, Last save, Save, Options, Credits, Disconnect, Exit), `menu_network_game`. Button names the script needs: btn_net_game, btn_options, btn_credits, btn_quit, btn_multiplayer, btn_logout, btn_ret, btn_lastsave, btn_save, btn_quit_to_mm, btn_internet, btn_localnet, btn_new_back.
- The menu works in a 1080 high space scaled to the screen (anchors `fullscreen` / `mid-center` keep it undistorted at 4:3 and 16:9). Window rects, `SetWndPos` and the mouse are real screen pixels.

## Original SoC menu (Steam SoC 1.0006, read with the archive reader)
- `ui_mm_main.xml`: static wall picture `ui_menu_backgraund` (1024x768, with a transparent hole top right), behind it the looping video `ui_mm_main_back_crop.ogm` (876x400 at 148,0: rainy sky and the Chernobyl power plant), `ui_mm_fire.ogm` (128x256 at 525,459, the burning fire), and the "shniaga": a metal bar (`ui_menu_move`, glass pane) that glides to the button under the cursor, two gears (20 frame animation) and two gratings at its ends. Black `graffiti22` button texts sit on the metal shutters of the picture.
- The Steam SoC archives have an obfuscated index (trivial_encryptor, then LZ-Huffman), data is LZO. The old `xdb.py` cannot read them. A reader was written for this task: `tools\xdb_soc.py` (key parameters from the open XRay-1.0006 sources).

## What this patch does
- `gamedata\textures\ui\socfx_mm_sheet.dds` = the original SoC `ui_mainmenu.dds` (wall, gears, bar, gratings); `socfx_mm_back.ogm` and `socfx_mm_fire.ogm` = the original SoC videos (renamed so nothing of CoP / xrMPE / the mod is touched). `socfx_mm_left/right.dds` fade the picture out on screens wider than 4:3 (off screen at 4:3), `socfx_mm_clear.dds` is a transparent texture for the buttons.
- `configs\ui\textures_descr\socfx_mm.xml`: the SoC texture list with ids renamed `ui_menu_*` -> `socfx_mm_*` (generated by `tools\gen_textures_descr.py`).
- `configs\ui\ui_mm_main.xml` (generated by `tools\gen_ui_mm_main.py` from the mod's own xml): every SoC number x 1080/768; black fill, video, wall picture, fire video, side strips; the five pages with the SAME buttons, window names, caption strings and music as before, now transparent black graffiti22 text, 551 wide and 49 per row (the pitch of the shutters of the picture, so each text sits on its own plate; SoC rows were 30 x 1.40625 = 42 and drifted off the plates); version text bottom left in the SoC style. The mod logo and the "OPEN BETA TEST" label are gone (the wall already shows S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Shadow of Chernobyl).
- The selector bar. The engine ignores a `<shniaga>` bar in this xml (tried: nothing drawn), so `scripts\socfx_mm_selector.script` builds it from plain statics: bar, two gears (frames turn while it moves), two gratings. The bar is drawn at 0.65 of the SoC size (BAR_F, in the generator and in the script) and its glass is centred on the row, so its frame does not cover the buttons above and below. It follows the mouse (button row under the cursor, glides at the SoC speed, hides when the menu buttons are hidden) and is wrapped in pcall (on any error the menu works, only the bar is missing).
- `scripts\ui_main_menu.script`: the mod's script unchanged plus a block at the end (tools\hooks_append.lua) that creates the selector, feeds it the mouse and updates it. It is the only patch that ships this file.
- Left as it was: Options / Load / Multiplayer dialogs and every other menu, the strings, the menu music (`music\menu`, SoC track from `soc_music`).

## Not possible / differences
- The SoC "magnifier" (the glass enlarges the button text) is a special engine render pass; the glass pane here is a plain semi transparent texture, the text is not magnified.
- Widescreen: SoC stretched its 1024x768 picture; this patch keeps the picture undistorted and fades the sides (our own strips, not original art).
- The bar following a real mouse was checked by the harness with a simulated mouse move in the script (OnMouse takes pixels like all window rects here); no human played with a real mouse.

## Test (automated, Cordon, one client, `tests\`)
- `tests\mm_screenshot_hook.lua` opens the menu with `main_menu on`; `tests\menu_pages_test_only.lua` (test only, appended to the script in a throw-away stage) moves the bar and switches through all five pages and asks the harness for an engine screenshot of each.
- 800x600 (4:3) and 1280x720 (16:9, `-Cfg vid=1280x720`, vid_mode + vid_restart): all pages show all their buttons on the shutters, the bar sits over the chosen row, the video plays, client log has no missing texture / xml / script error. Screenshots in `tests\` (v2_*: after the bar was resized so it no longer covers the neighbouring rows).

## Кратко (RU)
Главное меню приведено к оригинальному ТЧ: кирпичная стена с надписью S.T.A.L.K.E.R., анимированное небо с ЧАЭС и огонь (оригинальные видео ТЧ), металлическая планка выбора с шестерёнками и решётками, чёрные кнопки на металлических пластинах. Все кнопки xrMPE (Play, Options, Credits, Exit, Return, Last save, Save, Disconnect, Via the Internet / LAN) на месте. Работает в 4:3 и 16:9. Лупа (увеличение текста) в движке и не переносится. Логотип «Shadow of COOP» и надпись «OPEN BETA TEST» убраны.
