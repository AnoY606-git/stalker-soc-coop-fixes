# Cordon opening in the original SoC style (cutscenes_pkg_opening + cordon level.game)

## What players reported (new Cordon game, v1.3)
1. The Marked One does not spawn at Sidorovich's right away; you walk a few metres and only then are put there.
2. No intro and no Sidorovich animation at the start, the first task has no description.
3. Another group: no spawn there either, the chain could be started, the tasks were not shown until the flash drive was handed in,
   the messages looked wrong. They asked for the ORIGINAL SoC opening dialog with only the "Давай как с опытным" answer.
4. Sidorovich's dialog listed "Здравствуйте, вызывали?" (the mod's own start), the work lines and "Пока".

## Findings (same scenario, listen host + 1 client, Cordon, engine screenshots and test lines)
| | stock mod | our v1.3 | this patch |
|---|---|---|---|
| spawn | co-op points in the village, (-190, -19.5, -135), 57 m from Sidorovich, he is offline | same point, the opening scene (fallback, no movie in the add-on) moves the player into the bunker about 8 to 15 s later | co-op points are inside the bunker, in front of Sidorovich, facing him, from the first frame |
| start tasks | none in 75 s (the restrictor waits `on_game_timer = 10` game seconds; its section stayed `sr_idle@start_quest` in every run, the game clock barely advances) | none in 75 s (our change additionally waited for the movie flag or 6000 game seconds) | both tasks at once (given by script), with title and description |
| Sidorovich | offline until you walk there | offline at the start, online only after the move | online and animating at the start |
| start dialog | "Hello, you called?" | same | SoC phrase 3, only the answer "Just give me a mission" / "Давай как с опытным" |

Causes:
- `levels\coop_l01_escape\level.game`: the four co-op spawn points (team 4, game type 128) are in the village. Our opening scene tried to hide that with a teleport after the movie, but the movie is not in the add-on, so it was a visible jump.
- `esc_start_main_quest_task_space_restrictor.ltx` gives the tasks from an `on_game_timer` rule; game-time timers hardly run in this mod (see stalker.md), so even the stock logic is late. Our v1.3 copy made it worse: it waited for `SCN_INTRO_PLAYED` or `on_game_timer = 6000`.
- `xr_effects.give_task` (base script) hands a task to the FIRST player only and returns, and `client_tasks.give_task_to_client` returns early when the host already has the task. The other players never got the start chain at all.
- The scene `intro` waited up to 45 s for players to leave the lobby area and 8 s more.

## What changed
- `cordon\gamedata\levels\coop_l01_escape\level.game`: the four team-4 co-op spawn points moved into the bunker (AI-node checked, 0.16 to 0.29 m), yaw -1.735 = facing Sidorovich, at least 1.4 m apart (like the stock Cordon points). A first try with 0.7 m spacing let only ONE of two dedicated-server clients spawn: the engine gives a spawn point only while no player stands near it. Lobby/spectator points and game types 1/140 are untouched. Deaths respawn in the bunker too (safe place; same points).
- `dialogs_coop_l01_escape.xml` (`cordon`, and the `quest_dialogs` copy that is built on it): the Sidorovich start dialog is now the SoC `escape_trader_start_dialog` structure. Until the flag `COOP_SOC_OPENING_TALKED` exists he says SoC phrase 3 ("The choice is yours...") and the only answer is 32 ("Just give me a mission", RU "Давай как с опытным."), then 321 (find Nimble / Шустрый, flash drive, ask Wolf), 3211 "Go on.", 32111, 321111 and the window closes. The flags that completed "Hello, you called?" are given at phrase 321 (not at the end), so a cut-off dialog cannot block the chain: `ESC_START_TALK_TO_THE_SIDOROVICH_QUEST_TASK_INFO` (completes "Important mission", gives the flash-drive task and the Wolf task) and `...TALK_TO_THE_WOLF_STOP_DIALOG_INFO` (hides the old "Hello, you called?" option). Afterwards he greets with the mod's usual line. Texts are the original SoC strings (`configs\text\eng|rus\st_coop_soc_opening.xml`), voice lines 3 and 321 are the original SoC ogg files under `sounds\characters_voice\dialogs`.
- `coop_soc_opening.script`: dialog glue (PDA archive line for every player, original voice for the talking player) and the start chain tick. Every second the server gives each task to each player once while its stage is open: "Начало начал" until `all_good_boy`, "Важное поручение" (talk) until the talk flag, then the flash-drive task until it is handed in and the Wolf task until Wolf is talked to. The engine ignores a task a player already has, late joiners and respawned players get them too.
- `esc_start_main_quest_task_space_restrictor.ltx`: stock start logic again (`on_game_timer = 10`: later also the autosave and the helicopter chain), with the helicopter fallback kept. The tasks no longer depend on it, the script above gives them.
- `coop_scn_opening.script`: movie `intro` starts 1.5 s after the first player is in (was 8 s) and waits 10 s (was 45 s) for stragglers; a player is moved to the bunker only when more than 6 m away (movie path). Sidorovich's monologue, hello and bye lines and hints are unchanged, and the original voice files for them are now shipped (`sounds\script_replics\trader`, `sounds\characters_voice\scenario\trader`, 1.7 MB total), so the monologue is voiced instead of subtitles only.

## Not changed / limits
- Sidorovich in the mod is a stalker model with the CoP sitting animation set (animpoint, four random idles); the SoC trader skeleton animations (`vstuplenie`, `chair_hack_*`) are not in the add-on and cannot be driven on this model. He is online and plays his sit idles from the first frame; the SoC "lean to the desk" is not reproduced.
- Only the experienced branch exists (as the players asked); the tutorial branch (pistol and knife hand-out, PDA tutorial dialog) is not offered. (The scripted PDA walkthrough that was offered after the monologue was removed later, see the update section at the end.)
- The lobby READY button cannot be driven by the harness; players spawn through `game.respawn_dead_players()` (same spawn points as the real start). One real READY run is the only thing not covered.
- Flags are shared: the first player who talks gets the SoC opening, the others later see the normal greeting (same as the mod's other quest dialogs).

## Update 2026-10-21 (maintainer feedback on v1.4): spawn in front of the counter, no PDA prompt, no subtitles, voice language per player
This section replaces the older statements above about the spawn place ("in front of Sidorovich" at x -247.1 and the first room), the PDA tutorial offer, the subtitles and the hint pop-ups.

### 1. Spawn: the room in front of the counter, not behind the door
Measured on the collision mesh (`level.cform`) and the AI map of the mod Cordon (tool scripts were throw-away, numbers below are what matters):
- The bunker has two parts. West of the sliding door (`esc_trader_door`, wall at x -248.2, doorway z -134.9 to -133.7) is the first room (AI nodes x -250.6 to -249.2). East of the door is Sidorovich's room (x -248.2 to -241, z -135.9 to -132.7).
- A counter (the desk with the notebook, radio and lamp) stands across that room at x -246.0 to -245.7 and blocks it from wall to wall (no AI node at x -245.7 in any row). Sidorovich sits behind it at (-244.7, -24.8, -134.0), cover `esc_smart_terrain_1_animpoint_3`. Players talk to him from the WEST side of the counter.
- The v1.4 points were (-247.1, -135.1), (-247.1, -133.7) and two in the first room (-248.5 and -249.9, outside the door). The engine picks one of the points, so in about half of the starts the player appeared behind the door (the proof log of the first test shows a spawn at -249.9).
- Now: four team-4 points, ALL in the room in front of the counter, facing Sidorovich (yaw from the point to him): (-246.4, -133.7), (-246.4, -135.1), (-247.8, -134.4) and (-247.1, -134.4), floor y -24.8, every point on or within 0.13 m of an AI node (`tools/gen_level_game.py <in> <out> <level.ai>` checks it). The first three are at least 1.4 m apart (stock Cordon spacing). The fourth is the best that fits: the free floor in front of the counter is only about 2.2 x 2.6 m (cabinet at the north wall, a box at the south wall, the counter in the east), so a fourth player stands 0.7 to 1.0 m from the others.
- `BUNKER` in `coop_scn_opening.script` (late joiners, the movie path) is now (-246.4, -24.8, -134.4), also in that room.

### 2. The PDA tutorial question is gone
The yes / no box "Sidorovich can explain how to use the PDA. Listen?" was created at the end of Sidorovich's intro by `offer_pda_tutorial_client()` (a `fn` step of scene `sidor_intro`, `ui_test_dialog.show_callbacks("message_box_yes_no", ...)`). In SoC the "treat me as experienced" branch skips the tutorial, so the whole path is removed: the step, the box, the server callback `coop_opening_pda_yes`, the scene `pda_tutorial`, its 15 voice pages and the string. Nothing waited for the answer (the start tasks come from the server tick in `coop_soc_opening.script`, the box was the last step of the scene), so the chain is unchanged.

### 3. No subtitles and no hint pop-ups
xrMPE will get its own subtitle feature later, so the opening draws no text for Sidorovich's voice lines now. Also removed (all of them came from this patch, scene `hints` and the `fn` tip of `sidor_intro`; nothing else in the mod shows them): the newbie hints (jump, crouch, "hold sprint", "binoculars (5)", relations) that started at the bunker door and the "to talk, aim and press use" tip. The generated files `configs\ui\coop_scn_opening.xml` and `configs\text\<lang>\st_coop_scn_opening.xml` and `tools/gen_opening_text.py` are deleted (the empty stub of `cutscenes_core` applies again). The subtitle source texts stay in `tools/opening_text.json` for the day the engine feature exists. A line with no recording in the chosen language is simply silent and nothing waits for it. The dialog window text (the SoC start dialog) is the normal talk UI and is unchanged.

### 4. Voice language per player: `voice_language = auto | rus | eng`
- Setting: key `voice_language` in the new `[client]` section of the player's OWN `coop_settings.ltx` (patch `host_settings`, `coop_host_cfg.client_str`). It is read on every machine, also on a client that only joins; the file (or the section in an older file) is created at the first start with an EN and a RU comment. Default `auto` = follow the game language (xrMPE `InstallLang`: Cyrillic option texts = Russian, else English). A bad value logs `SOCOPEN VOICE bad voice_language` and uses auto. Read once per Lua VM, so a change applies at the next level start.
- The scene steps are built on each client (`coop_scenes.begin_run`), so each player hears HIS language; the choice is `coop_soc_opening.voice_lang()`. Log lines: `SOCOPEN VOICE setting=<s> game=<g> -> <lang>` once, `SCENE opening SAY <line> lang=<l> audio=<bool> path=<sound path>` per line, `SOCOPEN VOICE_PLAY key=3 lang=eng path=...` / `SOCOPEN NOVOICE key=3 lang=rus (no recording)` for the dialog.
- Files: Russian SoC recordings (from the CoP-port add-on) at the stock paths `sounds\script_replics\trader\*.ogg` (11 lines: monolog 1 and 2, the "come here" line, 5 hellos, 2 byes). English Steam SoC recordings of the same 11 lines under `sounds\script_replics\trader\eng\`, plus the two start dialog lines (phrase 3, 321) under `sounds\characters_voice\dialogs\eng\` (the Russian recordings of these two are at the stock path `sounds\characters_voice\dialogs\` in patch `soc_ru_voices`).
- Timing per language: the `ms` values in `coop_scn_opening.script` are the ogg length (granule position / sample rate) plus 300 ms, measured with `tools/ogg_ms.py` (Russian monolog 26.7 s and 47.7 s, English 23.7 s and 30.0 s). The scene `sidor_intro` is 90 s long (the Russian version needs about 88 s).
- Size: +1.3 MB of English files, minus the PDA tutorial pages. Budget check `python build.py <out>\b.db --skip=soc_levels,weapon_upgrades,npc_attachments,arrival_facing`: BUDGET OK, headroom 25.6 MB.
