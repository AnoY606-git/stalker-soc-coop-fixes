# Power plant (CNPP-1): "Operation Monolith" of the original SoC (phase 2, last missing task)

Request (maintainer FOSTER): "the quests themselves must be remade to match the original SoC". `patches\soc_aes_quests` did the chain up to the Control Monolith and listed "Operation Monolith" (`aes_military_task`) as impossible because "the mod has no military squads". That was wrong, see the first table. This patch adds the task with the SoC objectives, the SoC "Military action plan", map markers on the army groups and the SoC radio.

Planned changes line (EN, goes into `installer\changes_1.4.en.txt` and `changes_1.4.1.en.txt` (every `changes_1.4*` list that has the soc_aes_quests line) by `tools\apply_shared.py`): "Operation Monolith" from the original on the power plant level (CNPP-1). The military action plan (the "Situation" article) is found where the wounded soldier lay in the original, at the entrance. After that every player gets the task "Operation Monolith" with the original steps (the gate, the two Monolith fortifications, break through to the sarcophagus) and map markers on the gate, the fortifications, the sarcophagus and the army groups Sapsan, Korshun, Yastreb, Pustelga, Sokol, Grif, Sova and Lun (the army squads the mod already has on that level). The original radio reports of the battle play along the road (the original voice lines, in English or Russian like the voice language of the player, played for each player when he reaches the place). The task is done at the sarcophagus and never blocks the way.

Planned changes line (RU, same file `.ru.txt`): "Операция Монолит" из оригинала на уровне ЧАЭС (ЧАЭС-1). План действий военных (запись "Обстановка") находится там, где в оригинале лежал раненый солдат у входа. После этого каждый игрок получает задание "Операция Монолит" с шагами оригинала (ворота, два укрепления Монолита, прорваться к саркофагу) и метками на воротах, укреплениях, саркофаге и группах "Сапсан", "Коршун", "Ястреб", "Пустельга", "Сокол", "Гриф", "Сова" и "Лунь" (отряды армии, которые уже есть в моде на этом уровне). По дороге звучат оригинальные радиопереговоры боя (оригинальные реплики на английском или русском, как в настройке голоса игрока, каждому игроку, когда он доходит до места). Задание выполняется у саркофага и никогда не мешает пройти.

## How it works
`coop_soc_aes_military.script` (loaded by `coop_scn_soc_aes_military.script`, like every `coop_scn_*`): a tick (1 s, real time) on the server and (0.5 s) on every client, same pattern as `coop_soc_aes.script`.
1. `progress()`: flags `SOC_AESM_PLAN`, `_GATE`, `_MONO1`, `_MONO2`, `_FOUND` from the positions of the living players on CNPP-1 (and from the Sarcophagus arrival flags `AES_1_START_GO_TO_SARCOFAG_QUEST_TASK_INFO` / `SOC_AES_SAR_ARRIVED`, read only). Monotonic.
2. The task is given once per player while `PLAN and not FOUND`; the task section (`tm_coop_l11_stancia_1.ltx`) picks its description by the flags (`descr = {+MONO2} .., {+MONO1} .., {+GATE} .., default`) and its map target is the level changer (`aes_1_level_changer`, like the mod's own "reach the sarcophagus" task); `condlist_0 = {+SOC_AESM_FOUND} complete`.
3. `spots_step()`: carriers by story id (`get_story_object_id` of the squad, first living member via `squad_members()`), by `sim_board:get_smart_by_name` or by `alife():object(name)`. A carrier that does not exist is skipped (never an error, never a blocked task).
4. Client tick: the radio (zones of the local player, per-player flags `SOC_AESM_R_<NAME>_<zone>`, queue, the file of the voice language of this player `eng\` / `rus\`, `sound_object` 2D like the opening scenes, file checked with `getFS():exist`) and the "Situation" news (once per player, `SOC_AESM_NEWS_<NAME>`).
No step needs a living soldier; a dead group, a missed plan, a teleport, a player who left and came back never block anything.

## Not done / impossible
- **Army squads by script**: not needed, the mod has them (first table). Spawning more would double the garrison and needs free smart terrain jobs (a missing job slot aborts the server, see `player_scaling` NOTES); without a game run this is a crash risk for no gain.
- **The wounded soldier "Vasil Luckless" and a plan item**: the SoC soldier has a name string ("Vasil Luckless") and a dialog name `aes_sergeant_diallog` that is only referenced in a commented logic block of the level.spawn (no such dialog is defined). A new NPC or item section needs entries in system.ltx includes owned by other patches, and the plan would need a new inventory item. The plan is therefore a spot and a news text, not an object (the mod's own dead soldiers lie next to the spot).
- **BTRs (`aes_btr*`) and the SoC helicopter combat**: not in this patch. Done in v1.4.3 by patch `soc_aes_war` (one BTR, fighting helicopters, no all.spawn edit).
- **The SoC radio chain as the NPCs drive it**: the SoC infos are set by dozens of NPC `sig` events; they are mapped to the nearest of the SoC trigger zones instead. The line order is the SoC one. SoC has no subtitles for these lines. 31 of the 52 lines are not in the patch (size, see Size).
- **The SoC blowout timer** (`aes_timer_*`, `sr_aes_deadzone`, the lines `aes_blowout_*`): the SoC level has a timed emission; the mod has its own surge on this level (`cnpp_shake`). Not changed, its lines are not shipped.
- **Map markers are on the nearest mod objects**, 6 to 28 m from the SoC points (the SoC markers sit on `map_spot` restrictors that the mod's all.spawn does not have). A spot needs an existing object id.
- **Army relations to the players** are the mod's own (community `military` / faction `army`, hostile as in the SoC relation table); not changed.
- **The "heard" flags of a remote client**: the radio and the "Situation" article are once per player by a story flag that the CLIENT sets (`SOC_AESM_R_<NAME>_<zone>`, `SOC_AESM_NEWS_<NAME>`). On the host it is saved with the game; whether `story_flags.give_flag` on a remote client survives a rejoin of the level could not be checked offline (the story flags are engine side and the server sends them to the clients, a client change may stay local). Worst case: the lines and the article play again after a rejoin. The hook below prints the flags.
- Not verified in the game (offline task): `alife():object(name)` for the Monolith 2 carrier (the marker is skipped if it does not work), the 2D radio playback in both languages, the news text length. The hook below drives all of it.
