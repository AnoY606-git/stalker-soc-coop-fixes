# Army Warehouses: the snitch Pavlik and the Bulldog 6 order of the original SoC (phase 2, last two jobs of the level)

Request (maintainer FOSTER): "the quests themselves must be remade to match the original SoC". `soc_mil_quests` left two SoC jobs out because the mod has no NPCs for them: `mil_courier_job` (Lukash: the snitch Pavlik, 5 objectives) and `mil_freedom_rg6_task` (Bulldog 6 for Voronin, the Freedom Chef). This patch adds the NPCs at the SoC positions and both chains, and hooks the courier job into Lukash's SoC dialog of `soc_mil_quests`.

Status: code only, NOT run in the game (offline rules: no game launch). Offline checks and a game test hook are below.

## The original SoC chains (Steam SoC 1.0006: `tasks_military.xml`, `dialogs_military.xml`, `dialogs_bar.xml`, `level.spawn` / `level.game` of l07_military, `script_sound.ltx`; Russian text from the SoC add-on of the CoP port)
* `mil_courier_job` "Deal with the snitch": after the Duty job (and before the Barrier job) Lukash tells the player that Pavlik of Freedom is a snitch. Objectives: "Get to the farmstead" (marker on Pavlik), "Wait until the snitch brings out his agent", "Get rid of the snitch", "Get rid of the agent" (marker on Ara), "Report to Lukash about the mission". Lukash pays 7000 (`lukash_payment`), then offers the Barrier job. Pavlik (`mil_freedom_member0001`, profile `mil_Svoboda_stukach`) waits at `mil_freedom_courier_wait_path` (-178.6 -16.9 352.7). When the player is inside the restrictor `mil_bandit_restrictor` (sphere r 45.4 at -179.9 -16.1 364.8) he walks to the farmstead (`khutor_zone`, r 14.1 at -176.4 -16.1 378.2) and radios his agent (voice `mil_pavlik_to_ara`). If the player is within 12 m of Pavlik before that, he sees the player (`killactor`, objective 1 fails). The agent Ara (`mil_ara`, `mil_Killer_informator`) and three killer bodyguards (`mil_ara_guard1..3`) are spawned by the call, run in from the west village, Ara and Pavlik talk (`mil_ara_to_pavlik`; when Pavlik is already dead `mil_ara_ambush`), then Ara runs back west. Failures: Pavlik dies before his call, Pavlik leaves the farmstead zone, Ara gets away. The job is not repeatable.
* `mil_freedom_rg6_task` "Obtain a Bulldog 6 from Freedom" (the proof of loyalty for joining Duty): Voronin (`bar_dolg_leader_join`) sends the player to the Freedom base: "Find the cook in the Freedom base", "Talk to Chef" (the Chef gets drunk: five small talks, then three bottles of vodka, three questions; the third answer says the Bulldog 6 lies in the village), "Find Bulldog 6" (`wpn_rg-6`, story id `Mil_RG6` at -274.55 -27.47 245.41), "Bring the Bulldog 6 to General Voronin" (`bar_dolg_leader_quest_rg6_finish`: Voronin takes it and gives `dolg_scientific_outfit`; the oath `bar_dolg_leader_prisyaga` then makes the player a Duty member).
* Verified: Steam SoC has NO recorded voice for the dialogs of Lukash, the Chef or Voronin (the archives hold dialog voice for the Barkeep, the trader, the scientists and the Osoznanie only). The radio call, the talk of the two and the ambush shout of the courier job ARE recorded (`scenario\military\pavlik_morozov`, English in Steam SoC, Russian in the SoC add-on of the port).

## How it works
`coop_soc_milc.script` (loaded by `coop_scn_soc_milc.script`, like every `coop_scn_*`): a server tick (1 s) plus a client tick for the voice cues.
1. **Flags** (shared story flags, `MIL_SOC_*`, listed in `tools\milc_model.py`): `COURIER_START` (phrase 1501), `COURIER_KHUTOR` (a player inside the 45 m sphere), `COURIER_IN_KHUTOR` (Pavlik at the meeting point), `COURIER_CALL`, `COURIER_DEAD`, `ARA_ARRIVE`, `ARA_END`, `ARA_DEAD`, `ARA_LEAVE`, `COURIER_SEEN` / `COURIER_LEFT` / `COURIER_FAILED`, `COURIER_DONE` (phrase 5001); `RG6_START` (1111), `RG6_COOK_MET`, `RG6_ASK`, `RG6_GOT` (a player holds the weapon), `RG6_DONE` (hand-in); the Chef's small flags `MIL_SOC_COOK_DIALOG_1..6`, `_DRINK_1..3`, `_RG6_1..4`. `MIL_SOC_MADE_*` = the object was created once (never twice, a killed and released NPC is not replaced), `MIL_SOC_PAID_C7_<NAME>` / `MIL_SOC_PAID_SUIT_<NAME>` = paid once per player name.
2. **Phases of the courier job** (`courier_progress`, server): Pavlik is created when the job starts and a player is on the level. `COURIER_KHUTOR` -> his logic (`on_info`) sends him to the meeting point (the mod's path `mil_smart_terrain_1_walker_8_walk`) -> arrival (distance 5 m, or 45 s) -> the call cue (`MIL_SOC_CUE_CALL`) -> after 8.8 s (the length of the recording) `COURIER_CALL` -> Ara and the three bodyguards are created -> they run the way (`mil_smart_terrain_1_walker_6 / 5 / 4_walk`), `ARA_ARRIVE` by distance 7 m or after 80 s -> the talk cue (`CUE_MEET`, 9 s; `CUE_AMBUSH`, 4 s, when Pavlik is dead) -> `ARA_END`, Ara leaves along `mil_smart_terrain_1_walker_2_walk` -> `ARA_LEAVE` (job lost) when he is within 25 m of the far end or 110 s after the talk. A player within 12 m of Pavlik before `COURIER_KHUTOR`: `COURIER_SEEN` + the job is lost (Pavlik becomes enemy of that player, SoC `killactor`). Pavlik dead before the call: lost. Pavlik outside the zone (r 14.1 + 4 m) after he was in it: lost. Success: Pavlik and Ara dead after the call. No soft lock: a missing NPC counts as dead only after 4 s, Lukash dead closes the job without payment, the real-time timers (the game clock barely advances on the dedicated server) carry the story when Ara is frozen offline.
3. **Voice** (`CUES`): every client plays the cue flag once (baseline on its first tick: a load or a late join never replays) in ITS voice language (`coop_soc_opening.voice_lang`, English = `eng\` subfolder = Steam SoC, Russian = the SoC add-on of the port), 2D, only when its player is within 170 m of the farmstead; a missing file is silent. Files: `sounds\characters_voice\scenario\coop_l07_military\mil_soc_courier_call|meet|ambush.ogg` (+ `eng\`), 328 KB, the engine sound comment (v3) of the originals is kept.
4. **Bulldog 6** (`rg6_progress`): the Chef and the weapon are created when a player is on the level (SoC: always there). `RG6_COOK_MET` = a player within 12 m of the Chef, `RG6_GOT` = any player holds `wpn_rg-6`, monotonic (a later step also sets the earlier ones). The Chef dead before he talked: `RG6_ASK` is given (SoC fails the task, here it must not lock). Voronin dead: `RG6_DONE` once a player holds the weapon.
5. **Dialogs**: the NPC state comes from the flags only (`lukash_state` of this module layers `cpay / coffer / cwait / cfail` on the states of `soc_mil_quests`; exactly one greeting state holds for every flag combination, tested on 4000 random combinations). The Chef's and Voronin's dialogs are actor dialogs with dialog-level `has_flag` / `dont_has_flag` / `precondition`, like the mod's own.
6. **Placement**: the stored AI node and game vertex of every object are checked at run time (`level.vertex_position`), else the nearest valid node / game vertex is used; `tools\check_milc_chain.py --verify <coop.addon>` recomputes them from `level.ai` and `all.spawn` (all 7 confirmed).

## Not done / still impossible (and why)
* **Not run in the game** (offline task). The engine side that cannot be proven offline: that the engine reads `custom_data` of a runtime-created section (the script starts the same logic file after two idle ticks, the proven fallback of `cordon_technician`), that the walker schemes follow the mod's paths, and the online / offline behaviour of the created NPCs.
* **Ara and the bodyguards start 97 m west of the farmstead, not 200 m away as in SoC** (point 5 of the mod's paths `mil_smart_terrain_1_walker_6 / 5 / 4_walk`, on their own way). A standalone NPC made by `alife():create` does not move while it is offline (the server switch distance is 150 m, the players are at the farmstead), so the SoC start in the village would freeze him. If no player is within about 135 m of that point he stays offline: the timers still carry the story (arrival after 80 s, escape 110 s after the talk) and the "kill the agent" marker leads the player to him.
* **The SoC courier paths do not exist in the mod** (`mil_courier_walk1_2/3`, `mil_freedom_stukach_walk*`, `mil_freedom_khutor_center*`, the courier wait path; the Ara / bodyguard / Chef paths DO exist under `mil_smart_terrain_1_walker_*` / `mil_smart_terrain_11_walker_cook_*`). Pavlik waits where the engine puts him (the SoC wait point) and walks to the mod's bodyguard point `walker_8`, 10 m before the SoC end of his walk; no `all.spawn` edit was needed. After the meeting he stands there (SoC: relaxes at the farmstead fire, `mil_dolg_relax_dialog`, not ported).
* **Joining Duty is not part of this patch** (`bar_dolg_leader_prisyaga`, `actor_set_dolg`): the mod's `dialogs.actor_set_dolg` switches the community on the server's simulation board (`sim_board:set_actor_community`), not per player, and `soc_mil_quests` left `actor_set_freedom` out for the same reason. Voronin gives the order, takes the weapon and gives the suit (the SoC `enter_to_dolg`); the oath and the rest of the Duty line (`bar_dolg_leader_start` variants, the gate guard's 1000, the Freedom-leader kill job) belong to `soc_bar_duty_line`, which steps back for this Bulldog 6 job when the section `mil_soc_cook` exists and reads `MIL_SOC_RG6_DONE` as the Bulldog 6 step. The order of the two `apply_shared.py` does not matter (both insert `<actor_dialog>` lines into Voronin's block idempotently).
* The marker of "Find the cook in the Freedom base" points to the Chef (SoC: the Freedom base object `Mil_Freedom_base`, which the mod has no story id for). The SoC task fails when the Chef dies or the Duty attack explodes the base; here the hint is given instead (no lock).
* SoC resets Freedom to neutral when Pavlik dies (`mil_paul_frost_dead`); a kill may cost Freedom goodwill in the co-op engine (nothing replicated here). Pavlik's document item `mil_courier_doc` (commented out in SoC) is not made.
* Lukash, the Chef and Voronin have no voice in any language (the original has none); the courier job's cues play for everybody within 170 m of the farmstead as 2D sound (the original: a 3D NPC sound).
* Payments: 7000 and the suit go to every player once (project decision, SoC has one player); a second player is offered the same line (`5001` / the Voronin late line) until paid.
* **The mock test and the static checker of `soc_mil_quests` check the OLD Lukash dialog** (its model has no courier phrases and routes `3002 -> 7003`): after `apply_shared.py` its `tests\mock_test.py` and `tools\check_mil_chain.py` report failures about Lukash's phrases (Cap, Skull and Max logic are unchanged). Use this patch's `tests\mock_test.py` for the Lukash route: it plays the real dialog of the effective file, the courier job and the Barrier offer after it.
