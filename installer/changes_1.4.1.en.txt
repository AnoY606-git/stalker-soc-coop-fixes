New in v1.4.1 (all fixes since v1.4)

- @soc_economy: Knife: a stab with the left mouse button kills a typical bandit or stalker in 2 hits and the strong stab (right mouse button) in 1 hit (armored enemies need more); an NPC that strikes with a knife hits harder too
- @marked_one,bandit_jacket: The Clear Sky body armors (CS-1, CS-2, CS-3a) show the Clear Sky fighter models to other players again (the vest CS-2 had shown the exoskeleton before): each suit wears the body that matches its inventory model
- @cordon: Sidorovich's bunker door now opens for every player and stays open while anyone is inside the bunker (before, it fell shut again at once unless somebody stood at the top of the entrance stairs, so a player carrying the flash drive could not get in to hand it over)
- @repeatable_quests: Repeatable quests: fully in Russian; targets spawn in open ground inside the playable area, away from bases, safe zones and anomalies (bandits no longer appear next to Arnie in the Bar, inside the rookie village, or outside the border fence and beyond the army checkpoint), and the target squads stay at their spot; the map marker shows the job name and description and disappears for every player when the job ends
- @corpse_artifacts: The Battery artifact never dropped from dead stalkers in the original SoC (an error in its loot roll). Fixed: Battery and the 11 other rarest artifacts can be in the backpacks of dead stalkers on Radar, Pripyat and the Chernobyl NPP, 0.5% each per corpse, as SoC intended; the same for all players, rolled once per corpse
- @cutscenes_pkg_opening,cordon: Cordon start: the Marked One (the character with the star) spawns at once in the room in front of Sidorovich's counter (not behind his door), the other players start at the campfire of the rookie village, with the original SoC opening dialog (only the "treat me as experienced" answer), his opening speech is voiced, and every player gets the start tasks with descriptions. No tutorial pop-ups and no PDA tutorial question (the experienced branch skips the tutorial in SoC)
- @cutscenes_pkg_opening,host_settings: Voice language of Sidorovich's opening speech, chosen by each player: Russian or English SoC recordings, setting voice_language = auto | rus | eng in the [client] section of your own coop_settings.ltx (default auto = the language of the game; a line with no recording in that language is silent)
- @campfires: Campfires: stalkers no longer stand at a fire walking on the spot or on top of each other (the second fire of the rookie village, the Bar and Army Warehouses fires). A seat is now a free place of the circle around the fire that is not the barrel, not a stool or bench, and at least a metre from the other seats; a stalker who cannot get to his seat sits down where he stands. The Army Warehouses camp (10 stalkers) has a wider circle
- @cordon_technician: The Cordon technician now stands in the cellar under a rookie village house (wooden floor, iron bed, wall lamp), not in Sidorovich's bunker
- @no_tech_parts: Removed the technician parts that SoC does not have (box of transistors, can of rosin, pack of capacitors, coil of copper wire, textolite base): they are no longer lying in Cordon, Garbage and Agroprom, and the Bar and Army Warehouses technicians no longer ask for them (repair and upgrades are unchanged and never needed them). Items already in a backpack stay
- @soc_save_message: The "Game saved" message (quick save, autosave) is back at the bottom of the screen like in Shadow of Chernobyl instead of under the crosshair
- @repeatable_quests: Repeatable quests: bring-an-item jobs like in SoC: every artifact and mutant part order of the original task manager (23 jobs, in the original wording) at Sidorovich, the Barman, Sakharov and the Freedom leader Lukash, in the same "Do you have any work for me?" menu as the other jobs; as in SoC a giver offers one order of each kind at a time (artifact, mutant part), the next one when it is done, and some orders open with the story; paid to the player who hands in; the mod's own "I need work" task menu is gone (only the repeatable jobs remain); task icons are the SoC ones (the giver's face); the hand-in line only shows while you carry what a job asks for; Nimble no longer gives jobs (the pistol job moved to Wolf); a few extra task entries that SoC does not have (Rest on duty, Rescue Fox, Yurik, the measurement waiting steps) are no longer given
- @stash_markers: Storage boxes in the safe zones (Sidorovich's bunker and the rookie village on Cordon, the Duty checkpoint on Garbage, the Bar, Sakharov's bunker, the Freedom base) are marked on the PDA map from the start for every player, including late joiners; the mark stays when the box is opened
- @cordon,cutscenes_pkg_opening,repeatable_quests: Cordon after the flash drive as in the original game: Sidorovich gives the next story tasks right away (the Agroprom documents job, "Work for the trader", "Get to the railroad embankment", "Find the militaries' documents") for every player, in the original wording; the helicopter flyover and the extra tasks of the level maker ("Looks like something is up", the Fox detour) are gone, the soldiers at the bridge want 500 rubles for the pass instead of 5 bottles of vodka (any player can pay, the whole group may pass), and Sidorovich no longer speaks twice when you walk up to him
- @bar_arrival_anomalies: Bar: the anomalies that stood right where players arrive after a level transition (the gravitational anomaly and radiation fields in front of the Garbage entrance, the ones at the arrivals from Rostok and Wild Territory and in the yard) are removed, nothing stands within 30 m of an arrival point any more
- @soc_task_texts,darkvalley_restore,freedom_duty,sarcophagus_count: Task titles and descriptions in the PDA now use the original SoC wording, in Russian and English, for the story tasks of every level after Cordon (Garbage, Agroprom, Dark Valley, X-18, the Bar, Rostok, Military, X-16, Yantar, Radar, Pripyat, the Sarcophagus).
