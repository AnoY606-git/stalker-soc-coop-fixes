New in v1.4.3

- @cordon_technician: The technician in the rookie village cellar no longer appears outside behind a fence (v1.4.2 placed him on the surface above the cellar); he now stands deeper in the cellar on its floor, and saves from v1.4.2 move him there automatically
- @cordon: "We don't leave our own behind" (talk to Petruha) can no longer get stuck: if the ATP bandits are already dead, the task completes by itself and the next steps follow
- @repeatable_quests: Hand-in talk of the older item jobs (Wolf's lost pistol and four others): the lines are no longer swapped between you and the giver
- @cutscenes_pkg_yantar,cutscenes_core: Yantar, radiation measurements: the psi emission at the bus (the last measurement) now starts the original dream scene at the right moment for every player at once (screen blackout, the dream with the voices of Ghost and Klyk, waking up); before, it started as soon as anybody walked near the scientists' research stop. It plays once per game and never blocks the measurements or the quest
- @marked_one: Lobby: the balaclava stalker is back as the second character (after the Marked One); the six portraits are the Marked One and five partner faces as before
- @no_mp_items: Artifacts: the world model of placed artifacts now matches the original SoC one. Artifacts lying in the world (Vyvert, Medusa, Stone Flower, Night Star, Fireball, Moonlight, Flash, Sparkler, Soul, Mincer Meat, Blood, Goldfish, Crystal, Battery and others) kept the Call of Pripyat model baked into the level data, so the Vyvert lay on the ground as a beige bony star while its inventory icon was the SoC rock; they now use the SoC models. The Battery artifact got its own SoC model and texture instead of the Pustishka one, and the Fireball its original darker lava texture
- @cnpp_shake: Power plant (CNPP-1): the camera no longer shakes. The mod started a shaking effect when you walked into the level (v1.4.2 only turned it into 15-second waves); the original game has no shaking on this level, so it is gone completely. Saves made earlier are cleaned up on load
- @soc_aes_military: Power plant radio: 37 of the 52 original radio lines of the Monolith operation are in now (21 before): the helicopters and the first desant waves at the start, the gate, the monsters, the ambush at the bridge, the BTR ambush, the snipers and the commander at the end, each in the English or Russian original recording of the player's voice language, played to every player when he reaches the place. The radio and the "Situation" article now also work for the player who hosts the game
- @soc_aes_war: Power plant: the military helicopters and the army BTR are back. The 8 helicopters of the level used to fly their routes and never shoot; now they fly over the road, fire their machine gun at the Monolith soldiers they see, and as in the original they hunt the players only in the four zones where the original helicopters did (at most two at once); a helicopter that is shot down reports on the radio. The army BTR of the original drives up the road to the Sarcophagus entrance and covers it with its machine gun against the Monolith (it never shoots at players). Both can be switched off in configs\misc\coop_aes_war.ltx
- @garbage_soc_quests: Garbage: Seriy's radio call is back. Once you have finished Bes's job and have not talked to Seriy yet, every player hears the original message from the hangar (the bandits have gone completely wild) and gets the "Total Lawlessness" note in the PDA, once, as in the mod before. The patch removed it by mistake.
- @soc_economy: Traders also stock the items they sold in the original SoC and the mod lacked: the Barkeep PB, Colt 1911, L85, SIG 550, G36 and 5.56 ammo; Petrenko Walther, SIG P220, AKS-74U, Val, SVD and .45 hydroshock ammo; Freedom's trader G36 and exoskeleton; Sidorovich 9x18 PMM ammo. Sidorovich also sells the white leather jacket, and the leather coat he had in stock is now really for sale (it had no price, so he never sold it)
- @soc_economy: Detectors are no longer sold, as in the original SoC (traders did not sell them there). They are not needed to see artifacts: every artifact in the mod is visible without a detector, as in SoC
- @soc_savepack: Chapter save pack support: when a save of the save pack is loaded, every player (any name, also players who join later) gets the chapter's weapons, suit, ammo, medkits and money once
- @cutscenes_core,cutscenes_pkg_yantar,cutscenes_pkg_monolith: Cut-scenes play the Full HD movies (intro, dreams, all endings) when the cutscene pack's HD file is installed; without it the normal movies play as before
