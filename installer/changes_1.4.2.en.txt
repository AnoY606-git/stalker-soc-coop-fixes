New in v1.4.2

- @cutscenes_core: Game start: the old Call of Pripyat logo videos (GSC, GSC World Publishing, ATI, AMD) are gone; the game starts with the xrMPE intro again
- @bandit_jacket,marked_one: Body armors whose model already has a helmet or mask (CS-3a, PSZ-7, Bulat, Berill-5M, Healing Berill, Leather Trench Coat) now lock the helmet slot, like the exoskeletons, SEVA and gas-mask suits did before, so no second helmet is drawn through the built-in one
- @arena: Bar Arena: players no longer freeze after a round (before, the arena cutscene logic switched controls and HUD off again while the players were still in the pit and never switched them back, so they stood staring at the wall and were returned to Arnie's room frozen)
- @garbage_duty: Garbage, Duty checkpoint: without a pass, a player who walks through the checkpoint toward the Bar is first warned by the guards and then attacked, as in the original (only that player is attacked; once the pass is given nobody is)
- @garbage_soc_quests: Garbage: Seriy's 2500, Bes's 2000 and the Duty commander's 1500 are now paid to the talking player exactly once on every kind of game (before, the host player of a listen game was never paid)
- @cordon: Cordon: the last helicopter flyover before the flash drive is handed in is gone, as in the original
- @soc_ru_voices,cutscenes_pkg_yantar: Yantar: the voices of the dream in the scientists' dream scene now play (the original recording, Russian or English by the voice language)
- @bar_arrival_anomalies: Bar: only the anomaly right in front of the Garbage arrival is removed; the other nine anomalies that v1.4.1 removed are back, as in the original
- @garbage_gray: Garbage: the bandit attack on Seriy's group now starts as in the original, for any player (standing in the hangar area starts it after 30 seconds, the strip in front of Seriy or the bandits' camp starts it at once)
- @cutscenes_pkg_opening,cutscenes_core: Sidorovich cannot be talked to while his scripted speech or greeting is playing, so two recordings no longer overlap
- @cutscenes_pkg_opening,soc_dv_x18_quests,soc_yan_x16_quests: Choosing the next dialog answer cuts the line still playing and the next one starts, as in SoC (Sidorovich, the Barkeep, Sakharov, Kruglov)
- @soc_talk_ui: Talk window: no shade stripes at the top and bottom any more, and on 16:9 screens the frames keep the SoC proportions instead of being stretched sideways
- @soc_yan_semenov,soc_yan_x16_quests,quest_dialogs,repeatable_quests,yantar_restore: Yantar: the second scientist of the original. When the Wild Territory professor was not rescued, Sakharov now sends you with the assistant Semenov (a new NPC next to the bunker, the original talk) instead of Kruglov. After the three measurements Semenov lies wounded at the last site and can be talked to; if the zombies kill him, Sakharov asks you to come back ("Talk to Sakharov" with the PDA message) and gives the prototype anyway, as in the original. The wounded scientist and his talk exist only in this Semenov variant.
- @soc_aes_military: "Operation Monolith" from the original on the power plant level (CNPP-1). The military action plan (the "Situation" article) is found where the wounded soldier lay in the original, at the entrance. After that every player gets the task "Operation Monolith" with the original steps (the gate, the two Monolith fortifications, break through to the sarcophagus) and map markers on the gate, the fortifications, the sarcophagus and the army groups Sapsan, Korshun, Yastreb, Pustelga, Sokol, Grif, Sova and Lun (the army squads the mod already has on that level). The original radio reports of the battle play along the road (the original voice lines, in English or Russian like the voice language of the player, played for each player when he reaches the place). The task is done at the sarcophagus and never blocks the way
- @soc_doctor_chain,repeatable_quests: The Guide and the Doctor of the original SoC. Once you have taken Ghost's documents, the Cordon Guide has a new topic ("Guide, I need to see Doctor.", the original lines): the Doctor waits in Strelok's hiding place, "Meeting with Doctor" goes to every player and the Guide's PDA note shows. In the Agroprom underground the Doctor stands in that hiding place (his original spot), recognises Strelok, hears that Ghost is dead and hands over the task "Secret stash at Pripyat" (diary "Meeting with the Doctor"); the original English voice plays for his lines. The task leads to the decoder in the Pripyat hotel (see the entry above). Nothing blocks: a dead Doctor or a party that goes straight to Pripyat just skips the chain. Not in the mod: the Dead City (in the original you are knocked out in the underground and wake up in its jail), so the Doctor's jail talks and the Dead City quests are missing.
- @soc_bar_duty_line: The Duty / Freedom line of the Bar follows the original SoC. Duty's gate commander (Sergeant Plichko) and Captain Ivantsov stand where they stood in SoC; the commander sells the pass for 1000, Voronin, Petrenko and the commander speak the original lines. Join Duty: Ivantsov's test (win the pseudodog fight on the Arena), Voronin's Bulldog 6 job (the RG-6 lies in the Freedom village stash of the Army Warehouses), the oath and the Duty suit. The Barkeep's jobs "Kill the mad veterans" (Lucky and two veterans camp on the Wild Territory) and "Eliminate the Mercs" (Tyran with his men at the construction site, snipers on the way) end with their PDAs; after the Mercs Voronin sends you to kill the leader of Freedom (Lukash). Every task goes to every player, late joiners included; a dead NPC or a lost item does not block a step.
- @soc_mil_courier: Army Warehouses: two more jobs of the original SoC. "Deal with the snitch": after the Duty job Lukash tells you about Pavlik, a snitch of Freedom. Get to the farmstead without coming close to him, wait until he calls his agent, kill Pavlik, then the agent Ara (he runs away to the west, his three bodyguards come with him), report to Lukash for 7000. Lost, as in SoC, when you go within 12 m of Pavlik, kill him before his call, or Ara gets away. Pavlik, Ara and the Chef are new characters at the original positions; the two radio talks have the original voice (Russian and English, by the voice language setting). "Obtain a Bulldog 6 from Freedom": Voronin (Bar) sends you to the Freedom Chef, who talks after three bottles of vodka and says the Bulldog 6 lies in the village (it is there now); bring it to Voronin for the Duty scientific suit (every player gets one).
- @soc_stalker_talk: Ordinary stalkers have one question again, as in SoC: "Got anything interesting to tell me?", answered with one piece of news (instead of the Call of Pripyat menu)
- @cordon_technician: The technician stands deeper in the village cellar, away from the entrance (also moved in saves from v1.4.1)
- @soc_trade_ui: Trade window: darker grid like in SoC, and the middle column in the SoC rust colour
